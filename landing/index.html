ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built for Students. By BEU Developer.

BEU Exam Dost is an independent student-focused initiative created to make exam preparation resources easier to find, navigate, and use. Our goal is to provide a clean, practical digital companion for students preparing for Bihar Engineering University examinations.

We eliminate complex logins and paywalls. All study checklists, question banks, and timers are saved strictly in your device's browser memory so you can study whenever and wherever you want.

100% Student-Centric

Designed around how university students actually revise before end-semester exams.

Zero Data Collection

No personal account or tracking. Your progress stays locally on your phone.

Offline-First PWA

Install to your home screen in seconds and study seamlessly without an active connection.
